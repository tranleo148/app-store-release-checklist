# Mobile release checklists

Checklists for a first store release of a mobile app, native or cross-platform, in Vietnamese and English.

Read them on the site: https://tranleo148.github.io/app-store-release-checklist/ (App Store and Google Play tabs, VI / EN switch at the top)

| Checklist | Tiếng Việt | English |
|---|---|---|
| App Store (iOS) | [app-store/index.md](app-store/index.md) | [app-store/en.md](app-store/en.md) |
| Google Play (Android) | [google-play/index.md](google-play/index.md) | [google-play/en.md](google-play/en.md) |

Changelog: [`_data/changelog.yml`](_data/changelog.yml), shown on the site under each checklist title and at
https://tranleo148.github.io/app-store-release-checklist/changelog.html

Suggestions and fixes are welcome as issues or pull requests.
